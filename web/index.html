Vision Quality Inspection

An invented controller-board line with one camera at 30 frames a second. A patch-memory anomaly model trained on good boards and 24 labelled defects, deployed to an edge simulator; a supplier change, an operator's review, a retrained model as a canary, and a promotion gate, each scored against the simulation truth. …
Every image is rendered by a generator in this repository: no real line, camera or part. The generator knows which boards are defective; that truth is shown only to judge the model and is never used by it. The operator at the review station is a stand-in that labels from the truth with deliberate mistakes. No outside model or service is called.
